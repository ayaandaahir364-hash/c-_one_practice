# Chapter 3 – Gaadhista Go'aamada (Making Decisions)

Tusaalayaal code ah oo ka yimid Starting Out with Visual C# (Gaddis, 6aad), iyo sharaxaad kooban.

---

## 1. `if-else`

Haddii Temp ka weyn yahay 40, farriinta "hot" ayaa soo baxda; haddii kale, `else` ayaa shaqeeya. Tusaale: Temp = 90 → hot.

---

## 2. `try / catch` – Celceliska dhibcaha


Wuxuu helaa celceliska saddex dhibcood; haddii uu ka weyn yahay 95, hambalyo ayaa soo baxda. Haddii qoraal aan tiro ahayn la galiyo, `catch` wuxuu muujiyaa khalad.

---

## 3. Nested if – Deynta


Mushaharku waa inuu noqdaa 40,000+; kadib sannadaha shaqada waa inay noqdaan 2+. Labadaba haddii la buuxiyo, deynta waa la oggolyahay. Tusaale: 50,000 + 3 sano → waa u qalmaa.

---

## 4. `if-else-if` – Darajada (Grade)


Shuruudaha waa la hubiyaa kala horreyn; kan ugu horreeya ee run ah ayaa shaqeeya. 90+ = A, 80+ = B, 70+ = C, 60+ = D, kale = F.

---

## 5. `bool` iyo Flags

Doorsoomaha `bool` wuxuu haystaa `true` ama `false`. Halkan `isGrandMaster` waa `true` haddii dhibcuhu ka weyn yihiin 5000. Tusaale: 6000 → True.

---

## 6. `CompareTo` – Qoraallada


`CompareTo` wuxuu is-barbardhigaa labo qoraal; wuxuu soo celiyaa 0 haddii ay is le'eg yihiin. Tusaale: "Ali" iyo "Ali" → 0.

---

## 7. ListBox – Qoraalka la doortay


`listBox1.Text` wuxuu haystaa qoraalka shayga la doortay, MessageBox-na wuxuu muujiyaa. Tusaale: haddii la doorto Monday → your selected Monday.

---

## 8. ListBox – `Items.Count`


`Items.Count` wuxuu soo celiyaa imisa shay ayaa ListBox-ka ku jira. Tusaale: 7 maalmood → Total items is : 7.

---

## 9. ListBox – Tirtirka shayga


Marka hore wuxuu hubiyaa in wax la doortay (`SelectedIndex` ma aha -1), kadibna `RemoveAt` wuxuu tirtiraa. Haddii kale, farriin ayaa soo baxda.
